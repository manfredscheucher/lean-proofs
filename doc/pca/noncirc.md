# NonCirc: non-circularizability certificates

Libraries `NonCirc` (root `CombinatorialGeometry/NonCirc.lean`) and `NonCircCerts` (all generated
certificates), files `NonCirc/`. Independent of `Pca`. In progress.

## What is proved

For concrete arrangements of six pseudocircles, given by their fingerprints, there are no plane
circles realizing them. Each certificate `NonCirc.Cert.<Name>.not_circularizable` has the form

```lean
theorem not_circularizable :
    ¬ ∃ (c : ℕ → ℝ × ℝ) (r ε : ℕ → ℝ) (P : ℕ → ℕ → ℤ → ℝ × ℝ), F.IsCircleRealization c r ε P
```

where `F` is the fingerprint literal in the certificate file. In the root library: `N6Delta`,
`Missing6Open1129`, `Missing6Open1137`, `N63Core`; `NonCircCerts` builds all files in
`NonCirc/Cert/`. The first certificate, `NonCirc.N63.not_circularizable` (`N63.lean`), is a
hand-structured proof for the arrangement `N_6^3`; `N63Core` checks it again with the generic
pipeline.

## The statement

Only these definitions enter a certificate statement (`NonCirc/Fingerprint.lean`,
`NonCirc/Plane.lean`):

* `Fingerprint := List (List ℤ)`: for each circle the cyclic list of signed labels of the circles
  crossing it.
* `side`, `order`: the side of a crossing with respect to a third circle and the order of the
  crossings along a circle, read off the fingerprint.
* `IsCircleRealization F c r ε P`: circle `i` has centre `c i`, radius `r i > 0` and orientation
  `ε i = ±1`; every two circles cross; the crossing points `P j k s` lie on circles `j` and `k`,
  on the side of every other circle given by `F`, and in the cyclic order given by `F` along each
  circle.

`F_wellFormed` checks that the fingerprint is consistent, so the hypotheses are not
contradictory by themselves.

## How a certificate is checked

1. Plane circles and points are lifted to Lorentz space `ℝ^{3,1}` (`Lorentz.lean`), where the
   sign conditions become signs of determinants and inner products.
2. `Encode.lean` turns the fingerprint and a list of tags (instances of Grassmann–Plücker and
   related identities) into a CNF over three-valued signs; `encode_sound` proves that the actual
   signs of every realization satisfy it. No genericity is assumed.
3. The CNF is refuted by an LRAT proof stored in the certificate. `Checker.lean` is a verified
   LRAT checker (`check_sound`), evaluated in the kernel by `decide +kernel`.
4. `not_circularizable_of_check` (`Main.lean`) combines the two.

The certificate files are generated by `scripts/pca/noncirc_cert_to_lean.py`; the script and the
SAT solver are untrusted, Lean checks everything.

## Axioms

None beyond `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`. No `native_decide`.

## Source

Setting: S. Felsner, M. Scheucher, *Arrangements of Pseudocircles: On Circularizability*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.02149. The certificates are new and not yet published.

## Not formalized

* That a circle arrangement on the sphere realizing the fingerprint gives such plane data
  (stereographic projection from a point on no circle). The plane statement is the one proved.
* The fingerprint format here is not yet connected to `Pca/Fingerprint.lean` and the dart model.
